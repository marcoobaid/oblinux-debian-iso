## Live boot and session verification

The following checks passed:

- The OBLinux live GRUB presentation appeared in UEFI and legacy-BIOS modes.
- The boot background, symbol, `OBLinux Live` title, and theme colors rendered
  correctly and remained readable.
- The default live entry booted successfully.
- The utility and media-integrity entries remained available.
- Live-user autologin and GNOME startup completed normally.
- The Obsidian Horizon wallpaper remained the default.
- The live installer launcher displayed `Install OBLinux` with the OBLinux
  icon.

## Calamares verification

Calamares displayed the OBLinux product name, installer title, logo, welcome
image, sidebar palette, and `Installing OBLinux` slideshow. All previously
tested pages and controls remained functional. Canceling and relaunching the
installer worked correctly.

The sidebar was visually too dark. The bottom-left `About` control had
insufficient contrast and was difficult to see. This is an accessibility and
polish finding; the sidebar should be brightened within the approved palette.

## Installation and installed-system verification

The clean UEFI installation completed successfully. The installed system
booted, accepted login, and passed the repeated Build 004 wallpaper,
time-synchronization, timezone, APT, lock, suspend, and cleanup checks.

Post-install verification confirmed:

- No queried package name matched `calamares*` or `live-*`.
- `/etc/calamares/branding/debian/branding.desc` was absent.
- `/usr/share/applications/calamares-install-debian.desktop` was absent.
- The installed OBLinux wallpaper remained configurable and persistent.

These results confirm that package-owned OBLinux installer branding is removed
with `calamares-settings-debian` and does not leak into the installed system.

## Open branding findings

### GNOME About identity

GNOME Settings displayed the Debian logo and identified the operating system as
`Debian GNU/Linux 13 (trixie)`. A future OBLinux identity layer should display
the OBLinux logo and product name while accurately retaining Debian 13 as the
base system.

### Installed GRUB presentation

The generated installed entries displayed `OBLinux GNU/Linux` and
`Advanced options for OBLinux GNU/Linux`, rather than the desired concise
`OBLinux` presentation. The installed GRUB menu also retained Debian's default
visual theme. Live-media GRUB branding does not automatically configure the
installed system's GRUB theme.

### Plymouth startup presentation

After the installed GRUB menu, the early startup screen retained Debian 13
artwork. This is the installed Plymouth theme and is independent of the live
GRUB theme and GNOME wallpaper.

## Conclusion

Build 005 passes its functional boot, live-session, Calamares, installation,
cleanup, and regression acceptance checks. Its live boot and installer identity
are proven. Calamares contrast, GNOME About identity, installed GRUB identity,
and Plymouth branding remain explicit follow-up work.
